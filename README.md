# Fernando de Souza Bastos

**Professor of Statistics at the Federal University of Viçosa (UFV), Brazil**

I am a statistician and professor at the Department of Statistics of the Federal University of Viçosa (UFV). My work combines **statistical methodology, scientific software development, applied statistics, data science, and statistical education**.

My main research interests include sample selection models, computational statistics, statistical inference, non-Gaussian and heavy-tailed models, and applications of statistical methods to agricultural and biological sciences.

## Research Interests

- Sample Selection and Heckman-type Models
- Computational Statistics and Statistical Inference
- Non-Gaussian and Heavy-Tailed Models
- Statistical Modeling in Agricultural and Biological Sciences
- Data Science and Reproducible Research
- Statistical Education and Educational Technology

## Scientific Software

### [ssmodels](https://github.com/fsbmat-ufv/ssmodels)
R package for fitting classical and extended sample selection models, including Gaussian Heckman, Heckman-t, skew-normal, generalized Heckman, and Birnbaum-Saunders sample selection models.

### [heckmanGE](https://github.com/fsbmat-ufv/heckmanGE)
R package for generalized Heckman models with covariate-dependent dispersion and sample selection parameters.

### [fikzPy](https://github.com/fsbmat-ufv/fikzPy)
Python desktop application for converting raster images and line drawings into clean, editable TikZ code for LaTeX.

### [figchild](https://github.com/fsbmat-ufv/figchild)
LaTeX/TikZ package providing reusable illustrations for educational materials and children's activities.

### [stringartR](https://github.com/fsbmat-ufv/stringartR)
R tools for mathematical visualization and educational activities based on String Art.

### [rexamsconverter](https://github.com/fsbmat-ufv/rexamsconverter)
Tools connecting LaTeX documents and R/exams workflows for reproducible generation of educational and assessment materials.
